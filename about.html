About DIY Kit Index

DIY Kit Index is published by Shortell Designs. It compares consumer DIY test kits and exclusion products: charcoal vs continuous radon monitors, swab screen vs lab confirmation, mold kits that are not clearance, dryer-vent DIY vs hire, wildlife exclusion after vacancy, and why DIY asbestos sampling is discouraged.

Amazon Associates. As an Amazon Associate, Shortell Designs earns from qualifying purchases. Every product link on this site uses Special Links with tag radontestinde-20 and is marked (paid link). That disclosure also appears in the sitewide footer.

Methodology

  • Verified ASINs only. Products linked here already appear on live Shortell Designs how-to pages (radon, lead paint, mold assessor, chimney/dryer, wildlife). We do not invent ASINs or product names.
  • Method comparison, not spam. Each topic page explains what the method measures, where it fails, and when DIY is the wrong tool — so the content meets Amazon’s original-content expectation.
  • Official guidance first. EPA, HUD, NFPA, CSIA, and state wildlife rules are linked where they drive the advice. Consumer kits are screens or tools, not substitutes for certified inspections when those are required.
  • No invented labs or contractors. This site does not fabricate laboratory names, license numbers, or phone directories.
